# news-image-20261118 — "Tanker War"

Entry for make.fun **news-image-20261118**: "Sum up 18 November's world

The artwork is a newspaper front page for Wednesday 18 November 2026:
a burning Iranian oil tanker under a US destroyer's bow leads, under
the headline **TANKER WAR** — overnight the US sank five Iranian oil
tankers and Tehran struck back at Gulf shipping and a US base in
Jordan. Rows below cover the Kremlin's vow to rebuild the Kerch
Bridge, North Korea's ballistic missile launch, the COP31 climate
talks entering their decisive phase, and Nvidia's probe into claims
about its own CEO.
